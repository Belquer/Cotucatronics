## 1. The saga, factual

### 1.1 KiCad 10 unavailable → installed via AppImage (Stage 0)

The files are KiCad 10 (pcb format `20260206`, sch `20260306`) and **no
KiCad installable via apt/conda/PPA opens them** — it was the blocker that closed
the previous report. Resolved by the path that `/aipcb`'s **Stage 0** already
anticipates: `scripts/bootstrap_kicad.sh` pulls the **AppImage from GitHub**,
extracts the squashfs, and the pipeline **runs the version gate again**. It
passed: `kicad-cli` 10.0.6 opening, running ERC and DRC, routing, filling zones,
and exporting — the *"the file OPENS"* that Stage 0 requires, proven, not assumed.

### 1.2 The six criticals from the SWEEP — already fixed in the file

The six criticals C1–C6 that `VARREDURA-PRE-FABRICA.md` (the de-facto 001) caught
were already fixed **in the file** and still pass by measurement:
`funcao_dos_pads.py` gives **18 rules · 0 failures** (brain board) and **12 · 0**
(panel board), and `--sabota` fails when a correct pad is corrupted — the guard
has teeth. Nothing new here; the rev C work was copper geometry and placement.

### 1.3 C16↔J14 — a waiver DENIED by measurement

The brain board's only DRC **ERROR** was a `courtyards_overlap` between **C16**
(100n +3V3A, 0805, F side) and **J14** (DIN-41651 box-header, IDC 2×13). It looked
like a reasonable waiver — courtyards are nominal clearance. On measuring
**body-against-body**, C16 was **entirely inside** J14's F.Fab body (the 0805
under the shroud of a flush-seating header): a real collision, not clearance. **A
waiver is a measurement, not an opinion** — MUST-FIX, not SAFE-EMBARK. This case
is now the **anchor** of the Risk Certification Gate in `/aipcb` and the IFÁ10000
instance for hardware.

### 1.4 The J14 nudge was physically blocked → C16 to B.Cu + FreeRouting

The obvious fix — push J14 inward and give C16 a home next to the rail —
was **physically blocked by the preamp front-end** (D11/R11/J5) occupying the
only live interior corridor. With no room to relocate, the brain board solved it
by **taking C16 to B.Cu** and routing +3V3A through the plane strip with
**FreeRouting** (the router up: **JDK 25 + FreeRouting 2.4.1**, headless/offline).
Result: DRC errors **5 → 0**, and the 3 silk items dependent on C16/J14 resolved
as a knock-on. The generator was updated to the new state (not just the file).

### 1.5 The panel board took J1's real nudge (5,5 mm) — closed the latent C5

The panel board has the **same** IDC 2×13 (**J1**), the same class of failure,
and its +3V3A cap **C5** was ~0,5 mm from J1's body — one nudge from being the
next C16. Here relocation **was possible**: a real nudge of **J1 5,5 mm inward**,
re-routing of the corner, **C5 placed clean**. DRC 0 errors maintained. The same
defect on both boards, two outcomes — one worked around in copper because the
placement was stuck, the other **undone in the placement** because there was room.

### 1.6 Risk certification + ground review — both SAFE, no 🔴

- **Risk log** (output of the triple review): after clearing the ambient noise
  (`library not configured` came from a missing `fp-lib-table`, not the board),
  the real residue is **brain board 16 / panel board 5 warnings**, each with a
  **measured** verdict. The only ERROR was R1 (C16↔J14), already fixed. Everything
  else embarks with proof (metadata, the part's own silk, MPN-in-the-symbol).
  **GO** on both.
- **Ground review** (`revisao-terra` P0–P7): brain board with AGND/GND separated
  and a single bridge at NT1 (0 shorts ⇒ no loop), analog plane intact,
  decoupling pairs per rail; panel board with a correct single ground. **Both
  SAFE, no 🔴** — the only 🟠 is the decoupling distance of the ESP32 radio (C3/C4
  at ~10 cm), a noise question mitigated by the module's on-board caps, not a
  copper defect.

### 1.7 The congestion finding (Stage 3.5)

The **pre-layout** density review showed that J14 and J1 were the **same**
decision made twice: an IDC 2×13 pushed against the top edge, which kills one of
the two escape broadsides and over-allocates the corridor (**demand 17 > supply
13**). That metric predicts C16's collision **before any trace**. The finding
became a stage: `/aipcb` gained **Stage 3.5 — Congestion prediction** (GO /
REVIEW-PLACEMENT gate, two hard refusals), the reference `references/congestao.md`,
and the tool `scripts/congestao.py`. Run on the closed rev C, it still flags
**brain board J14 red** (the over-commit is real, it was worked around via B.Cu,
not undone) and **panel board J1 green** (the 5,5 mm nudge opened the second
broadside) — exactly the story of the two boards, measured.
